## Full Description (max 4000 chars)
BudFreeze lets you lock and unlock touch controls on your Bluetooth earbuds directly from your phone. No more accidental taps while working out, sleeping, or adjusting your earbuds.

WORKS WITH
- Realme Buds Air (1, 2, 3, 5 Pro, 7)
- Realme Buds Q, 2, 3
- OnePlus Buds, Buds 3, Buds Pro, Pro 2, Pro 3
- OnePlus Nord Buds, 2, 3
- OPPO Enco Buds, Buds3, W31, W51, X3, X3s, Air

Don't see your earbuds? Try BudFreeze anyway — it might work! If it does, report it from the app to help other users discover compatibility.

FEATURES
- Lock both earbuds at once or individually
- Quick Settings tile for instant lock/unlock without opening the app
- Find nearby earbuds using Bluetooth signal strength (supporter feature)
- Scan for any Bluetooth device nearby (supporter feature)
- Custom accent colors (supporter feature)
- Customize what each tap gesture does
- View earbud and case battery levels
- 100% free, no ads, no tracking

HOW IT WORKS
BudFreeze connects to your earbuds over Bluetooth and sends the same touch-control configuration that the official apps use. When you lock a bud, its touch controls are disabled until you unlock them.

IMPORTANT NOTES
- Pair your earbuds in your phone's Bluetooth settings first
- If you have the Realme Link app installed, force-stop it before using BudFreeze to avoid connection conflicts
- Keep earbuds out of the case during setup
- BudFreeze uses Bluetooth Classic RFCOMM, not BLE — this is why it works with these specific earbud models

PRIVACY
BudFreeze collects no data. No analytics, no tracking, no telemetry. Your Bluetooth connection stays between your phone and your earbuds. Full privacy policy is available in the app.

SUPPORTER

OPEN SOURCE
