# TouchLock Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 6, 2026**

TouchLock is an open source app that controls touch gestures on Realme, OnePlus, and OPPO earbuds via Bluetooth. This privacy policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

## Data We Collect

**TouchLock does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

The app operates entirely on your device. There is no server, no cloud service, no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising SDK.

## Permissions Explained

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `BLUETOOTH_SCAN` | Discover nearby earbuds for the Find Nearby radar feature |
| `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT` | Send touch lock/unlock commands to your earbuds via Bluetooth |
| `VIBRATE` | Haptic feedback when toggling touch lock |
| `BLUETOOTH` / `BLUETOOTH_ADMIN` | Legacy Bluetooth access for Android 11 and below |

No permission is used to collect data. All Bluetooth communication is directly between your phone and your earbuds. No data leaves your device.

## In-App Purchases

TouchLock offers a one-time "Supporter" purchase via Google Play Billing. The purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive a notification that the purchase was made (to unlock the supporter badge), but we do not receive or store your payment information. Google handles all payment processing according to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Data Storage

- **Earbuds pairing info**: Stored locally on your device using Android's Bluetooth system. Not transmitted anywhere.
- **Gesture settings**: Stored locally in your app's private storage. Not transmitted anywhere.
- **Touch lock state**: Stored locally in your app's private storage. Not transmitted anywhere.

## Open Source

TouchLock is fully open source. You can review the complete source code at: https://github.com/sarvesh/realme-buds-touchlock

## Children's Privacy

TouchLock is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page. Since the app collects no data, the policy is unlikely to change.

## Contact
